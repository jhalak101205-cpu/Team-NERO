RIGHT TO FAIR COMPENSATION AND TRANSPARENCY IN LAND ACQUISITION, REHABILITATION AND RESETTLEMENT ACT (RFCTLARR), 2013
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

1. OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE
The RFCTLARR Act 2013 regulates land acquisition by government and private entities in India. It ensures a humane, participatory, informed, and transparent process for land acquisition with just and fair compensation for affected families.

2. MANDATORY SOCIAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT (SIA)
Before any land acquisition process begins, a mandatory Social Impact Assessment (SIA) study must be conducted in consultation with the local Gram Sabha or Municipal body. The SIA evaluates:
- Whether the project serves a genuine public purpose.
- The minimum extent of land required for the project.
- The absolute number of affected families and displacement impact.

3. CONSENT REQUIREMENTS FOR ACQUISITION
- Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Projects: Requires consent of at least 70% of affected landowning families.
- Private Company Projects: Requires consent of at least 80% of affected landowning families.
- Direct Government Projects: Consent clause exempted for strategic infrastructure & defense.

4. COMPENSATORY RATES & MULTIPLIER FACTORS
- Rural Areas: Compensation is calculated as 2x to 4x the prevailing market value of the land parcel, plus a 100% Solatium (bonus).
- Urban Areas: Compensation is calculated as 2x the market value, plus a 100% Solatium.
- Timeline: Compensation awards must be deposited within 12 months of the final notification; failure to pay within 12 months lapses the acquisition process.
