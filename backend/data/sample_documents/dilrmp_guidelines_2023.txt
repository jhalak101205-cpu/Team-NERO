DIGITAL INDIA LAND RECORDS MODERNIZATION PROGRAMME (DILRMP) IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES (2023)
Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development

1. CORE COMPONENTS OF DILRMP
The flagship DILRMP scheme aims to build a modern, comprehensive, and transparent land records management system in India based on four pillars:
Component 1: Computerization of Record of Rights (RoR) text data.
Component 2: Digitization of Cadastral Maps and spatial-textual integration (GIS mapping).
Component 3: Modernization of Land Registration Offices (SROs) and auto-mutation.
Component 4: Setup of High-Accuracy Survey Infrastructure using DGPS, CORS networks, and LiDAR/Drone surveying.

2. QUANTITATIVE IMPACT ON LAND DISPUTE REDUCTION
National empirical studies conducted across 120 pilot districts reveal:
- Districts with >80% RoR-spatial integration witnessed a 42% reduction in fresh land dispute filings in revenue courts within 2 years.
- Auto-mutation on land sale registration reduced illegal double-titling fraud by 89%.
- The average time taken to obtain a certified copy of land record dropped from 14 days to under 3 minutes via online portals (e.g. Mahabhulekh, AnyRoR, Bhoomi, Jamabandi).

3. CHALLENGES IN STATE IMPLEMENTATION
- Spatial-Text Discrepancy: Discrepancy between textual area mentioned in RoR (e.g., 2.5 Acres) and actual polygon area measured on satellite map.
- Inter-Departmental Silos: Revenue, Registration, and Survey departments using isolated databases without real-time API syncing.
- Capacity & Training: Lack of trained surveyor manpower at the Taluka/Tehsil level for modern total station and drone tools.
